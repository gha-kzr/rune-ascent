# Spirits (fallen PvP heroes curse the other team) — decisions

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: built (2026-10-17). Goal: a player whose hero fell still plays (heroes fall after 4 to 5 turns
on average in AI-vs-AI PvP, about a third of them before the end), without making matches longer than they are.

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Multiplayer (PvP) only | Solo keeps its own balance | co-op PvE |
| Lobby rule | **Spirits**, a host setting like friendly fire, **on** by default | Compare matches with and without in real play | playtests settle it |
| Name | **Spirit** (*Esprit*); its actions are curses | "Ghost" is already a solo enemy | — |
| When it acts | **On its hero's own turn**, in the initiative order, as if it were still alive | Keeps the turn order players know; a fallen player has a moment of their own | waiting gets long again |
| Its turn | **10 s**, fixed, with a **Pass** button; it ends on the first curse | A spirit's turn is one choice, it must not bring the wait back | — |
| What it does | **One action per turn, chosen** among three: **Curse** (+20 % damage taken), **Root** (no movement), **Reveal** (ends stealth in a **cross of radius 3** around a chosen cell; an empty cross is a lost turn). **No heal**: a first version had one (12 HP on an ally); the AI's spirits healed nearly every turn and matches got about 10 % longer, so it was removed the same day | A real choice for the fallen player: hurt, hold or find. Curse is the only "+damage taken" in the game (the classes don't have one) | balance runs |
| Duration | **Until the spirit's next turn** (one full round) | The target plays its turn under it (Root needs that), every ally gets the +20 % | — |
| Range | **Anywhere on the board**, no line of sight | A spirit has no place on the board; simplest to understand | — |
| Stacking | **One curse per target at a time**: a target already under a spirit's curse can't be cursed again until it ends (greyed out) | Three spirits on one target would be +60 %; no instant executions | — |
| Visibility | **Seen by everyone**: an icon on the target, and a **message for all** at the cast ("A cursed B") | The cursed team can react (heal, step back) | — |
| Built as | **Spells**: the three actions are `SpellData` with 1 AP each; in classic combat the spirit has 1 AP and these three spells in its bar; in card combat its hand is always these 3 cards, one play (what it didn't play is thrown away and comes back: the choice is always whole) | Reuses targeting, previews, the replicated log, the AI and the balance stats; fits both modes with no special case in the rules of play | — |
| An absent or AI-played seat | The **AI plays the spirit**: Curse on the visible enemy with the fewest HP | A team must not be punished twice for a player who left | — |

## New pieces (for the design)

- A fallen hero stays in the turn order as a spirit (when the rule is on): no cell, no HP, untargetable, its turn is the
  spirit's.
- A **reveal** effect: ends stealth on enemies in the area (like being found).
- Two statuses: **Cursed** (DAMAGE_TAKEN_PERCENT +20) and **Rooted** (MP to 0), lasting until the spirit's next turn.
- The HUD: the spirit's bar (or hand) in place of the hero's, the 10 s countdown, Pass; the banner and feed line for every
  curse.

## How (as built)

- `BattleState.spirit_kit` (the roster's `spirit_spells`, set by `MatchState.make_battle` when the lobby's `spirits`
  setting is on; empty in solo): `Battle._report_deaths` turns a fallen unit into a spirit (`UnitState.become_spirit`:
  its `data` becomes a copy holding the kit, MP 0, statuses and cooldowns cleared) instead of removing it from the turn
  order. `UnitState.can_act()` (alive or a spirit) is what an action needs; `max_ap()` is 1 for a spirit. A spirit's
  turn has no ticks and no sudden death, and its action ends it (any action by a unit that isn't alive does).
- Durations count the target's turns, like every status: a Curse or Root lasts until the end of its target's next turn,
  which is within the round the decision asked for.
- `ApplyStatusEffect.exclusive` (one at a time on a target) and `RevealEffect` (ends stealth, `break_stealth`).
- `MatchSession.turn_limit()`: 10 s on a spirit's turn. `NetBattleController`: the banner, the prompt, and every
  spirit's cast told to all (banner and feed). `EnemyAI` plays a spirit without walking (a cast or a pass).
- Balance: `tools/pvp_balance.gd` plays with spirits (`spirits=0` without); the spell table shows "Class/Curse" and the
  like, and the summary counts spirit turns (at 10 s) apart from the heroes' turns.

## Measured (AI vs AI, 3v3, 160 matches)

- With the heal: about 10 spirit turns a match, the spirits healed nearly every time (9.6 heals of 12 HP a match) and
  matches got about 10 % longer in turns. The heal was removed.
- Curse, Root and Reveal: the AI roots nearly every time (7.7 a match), a rooted melee hero reaches nobody, and matches
  stay about 11 % longer in turns. Curse and Reveal only (`tools/pvp_balance.gd ... spirit=curse,reveal`): 2 % shorter.
  **Root is kept on purpose** (the owner's choice, 2026-10-17): to judge in real matches, where players won't root
  every turn; revisit if playtests find matches long again.
- The AI values a Curse at little (it expects 10 damage a turn), and it never reveals (it can't know where a hidden
  unit is): read its spell choice as a lower bound of what players will do.

## Open / deferred

  spell table) and in playtests.
- A spirit model or effect on the board (none for now: the target's icon and the message say it all).
